Just Orange CLI

Zero idle cost · Runs on your machine · Cache, history, and one perfect dish per run.

just-orange.sh 
# from the repo
cd Just_Orange/cli
./just-orange.sh

Ingredients: tomato, rice, onion, garlic
Taste: spicy · Prep ≤20m · Eat ≤25m
Allergies: none

Tomato Garlic Rice
• 1 cup rice        • 3 tomatoes
• 4 garlic cloves   • 1 onion
• Chili flakes      • Olive oil

1. Fry garlic + onion in oil until golden
2. Add chopped tomatoes, cook 5 min
3. Mix in rice + 2 cups water + chili
4. Simmer 12 min. Done.

Cached · gpt-4o-mini · sqlite

Quick start

Needs bash, curl, jq, sqlite3, and an OpenAI key for generative mode.

export OPENAI_API_KEY=sk-...
./just-orange.sh

What you get

  • Interactive menu — generate, view cache, stats
  • SQLite recipe cache (free, local, forever)
  • Allergy + exclusion filters
  • Token-capped gpt-4o-mini prompts
  • Retries, timeouts, clear errors

Core engine

Shared bash engine lives in core/recipe_engine.sh. Same prompt discipline as the Lambda path — cheap tokens, one recipe, strict format.
